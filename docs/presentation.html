The
Problem

Polymarket hosts hundreds of binary crypto prediction markets. The crowd sets prices — but the crowd is wrong in systematic, predictable ways.

Price trajectories carry strong predictive signal that participants consistently underweight when the clock is ticking.

No systematic edge detection

Retail participants rely on gut feeling. No one is running ML models on price trajectory data to find when the crowd is systematically mispricing an outcome.

Markets are not truly efficient

Short-horizon crypto markets (1h, 4h) show strong momentum patterns. Prices drift predictably toward resolution — but the market underweights this signal.

Flat betting destroys alpha

Even if you have an edge, betting the same amount on a 3% edge and a 12% edge wastes capital. Without proper sizing, positive-edge strategies still lose money.

Correlation is ignored

BTC and ETH move together. Betting on both without accounting for correlation is effectively doubling down on one direction — with no diversification benefit.

ProblemSolutionResults

1 — Per-Timeframe ML Models

Separate GBM model trained on each timeframe (1h, 4h, 1day, weekly). Each learns its window's specific dynamics.

2 — Probability Calibration

Isotonic regression post-hoc calibration. Raw GBM scores mapped to true probabilities. ECE reduced from 0.15 → ~0.000.

3 — Kelly Criterion Sizing

Bet proportionally to edge. Large edge → large bet. Small edge → small bet. Turns sub-50% win rate into positive ROI.

4 — Markowitz Penalty

Correlation matrix from CoinGecko data penalizes over-concentration in correlated coins. Forces real diversification.

The Look-Ahead
Bias Fix

Our original backtest showed +16.66% ROI and 56.1% win rate. These numbers were too good. We audited the code and found a critical bug.

The model was fed the full price history — including prices that occurred after we would have placed our bet. It was effectively seeing the future.

Kelly–Markowitz Optimizer

Kelly Criterion for bet sizing · Markowitz correlation penalty for diversification · SLSQP constrained optimization

Kelly Criterion

Computes the mathematically optimal fraction of bankroll to bet, given a calibrated edge. Maximizes long-run geometric growth rate.

f* = edge / (1 − market_price)
¼-Kelly (Risk 1–3) ½-Kelly (Risk 4–6) ¾-Kelly (Risk 7–8) Full-Kelly (Risk 9–10)

Markowitz Penalty

Penalizes allocating capital to correlated assets. Same-coin markets get correlation 0.95. Cross-coin pairs use CoinGecko 90-day correlations.

min −return + λ·(w·Σ·w)

At low risk: λ is large → forces diversification.
At high risk: λ → 0 → concentrates in best edge.

Eligibility Filters

Only markets that pass all four filters enter the optimizer.

edge > 2%Minimum alpha threshold
price 20%–80%Not near-certain outcomes
edge ≤ 15%Above = model error
not monthlyPoorly calibrated model

Live Output Example

BTC (1hour): edge +8.3% → Kelly 16.6% → Half-Kelly 8.3% → final weight 6.1% (after Markowitz penalty)

ETH (4hour): edge +5.1% → Kelly 10.2% → Half-Kelly 5.1% → final weight 4.8%

Cash reserve = 1 − Σ weights
