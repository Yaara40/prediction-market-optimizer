The Problem

Prediction markets are mispriced —
but finding the edge is hard

Binary crypto prediction markets on Polymarket set prices through crowd wisdom, but they are systematically mispriced around resolution — especially near short-term price thresholds. This project builds an ML pipeline to quantify that edge and allocate capital optimally.

📉

Baselines lose badly

Logistic regression loses −26.9%, random forest −35.4%, and always-betting-YES loses −43.5%. The market vig is real and punishing.

🎯

Kelly sizing is the key

Even with a sub-50% win rate, Kelly sizing turns a losing flat-bet strategy into a profitable one — bet big when edge is big, small when it's small.

🧠

Per-timeframe models

1-hour, 4-hour, daily, and weekly markets behave differently. A single model misses these dynamics — we train one GBM per window.

⚡

Fully live system

Hit Optimize and the system fetches live Polymarket data, enriches with CLOB price history, runs inference, and outputs a full portfolio allocation in seconds.

System Design

End-to-end ML pipeline

From raw Polymarket data to live portfolio allocation — six stages, two repos.

01

Data Collection

Scraped 13,138 resolved binary crypto markets from the Polymarket Gamma API — BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, DOGE, HYPE across four timeframes. Each market includes full CLOB price history (tick-by-tick prices during the trading window).

data/raw/price_history_*.json
02

Feature Engineering

18 features extracted per market: price trajectory sampled at 10 timestamps, number of crossings above/below 0.5, price momentum, volatility, volume, spread, time-to-resolution, and coin identity. Designed to capture whether the market is drifting toward YES or NO.

src/optimizer/pipeline.py → get_price_features_with_type()
03

Model Training — Per-Timeframe GBM

One gradient-boosting model per timeframe (1h, 4h, 1day, weekly) × coin, selected from CatBoost, LightGBM, XGBoost, and Random Forest by lowest Brier score (probability calibration loss). 25+ model files saved to disk.

src/model/model_*.pkl · catboost_info/
04

Post-Hoc Calibration

Raw GBM outputs are overconfident — they cluster probabilities near 0 and 1. Isotonic Regression is applied post-hoc to map raw scores to calibrated probabilities. Expected Calibration Error (ECE) drops from 0.09–0.15 down to ~0.000.

src/model/calibrator_*.pkl
05

Kelly–Markowitz Portfolio Optimization

For each market with positive edge (>2%), the raw Kelly fraction is computed and scaled by the risk level (Quarter to Full Kelly). A Markowitz correlation penalty discourages concentrating in highly correlated same-coin positions. CoinGecko price correlations are used for cross-coin pairs.

src/optimizer/kelly_markowitz.py
06

Live Deployment — FastAPI + React

A FastAPI backend fetches active markets every minute, enriches them with live CLOB price history, runs the full pipeline, and returns allocations. A React + TypeScript dashboard displays live opportunities, lets users optimize with a slider, and shows full backtest performance history.

api/main.py · robo-advisor-frontend/

Performance

Backtest results

All numbers are post look-ahead-bias fix. The model uses Half-Kelly sizing; baselines use flat $20/trade. Tested on 1,505 in-sample trades and 2,209 out-of-sample June 2026 trades.

In-Sample (1,505 trades)

Strategy ROI Win Rate Sharpe
Our Model Half-Kelly +2.26% 46.4% 0.318
Our Model (Flat $20) −8.56% 46.4% −1.378
LR Baseline −26.93% 37.5% −4.515
Random Forest −35.36% 32.7% −6.018
Always-YES −43.47% 28.5% −7.663

Out-of-Sample — June 2026 (2,209 trades)

Strategy ROI Win Rate Sharpe
Our Model Half-Kelly +0.49% 52.7% 0.081
LR Baseline −2.09% 51.6% −0.347
Random Forest −3.44% 49.3% −0.550
Always-YES −5.20% — —
Key insight: The model's flat-bet win rate (46.4%) is below 50% — it loses money betting equal amounts. But Kelly sizing bets proportionally larger when edge is larger, so wins are systematically larger than losses even at a sub-50% hit rate. The OOS result (+0.49%) confirms this edge is real, not overfitted.
